The Artist

Half artist,
half educator.

Gem built Hiddengemely on a simple belief: lashes are not an add-on, they're architecture. Working out of Carson, she maps every set to the face in front of her — never a copy-paste look, always a quiet kind of drama.

After years behind the chair, she turned her obsession with retention and shape into a curriculum. Today she's the creator of the Advanced Korean Lift + Extension™ and the founder of LA's first six-week lash mentorship — teaching in Spanish and English so the craft reaches the artists too often left out of it.

Her mission is the same in the studio and the classroom: leave people with more confidence than they walked in with.
