Pick a look

The same lashes can look very different depending on where the length goes. These are the four maps we use most, and we adjust each one to your eye shape.

Natural

Even length across the eye. It looks like your own lashes on a good day.

Works well with classic or hybrid

Doll

Longest in the middle, so the eye looks rounder and more open.

Works well with volume or hybrid

Cat eye

Shorter at the inner corner and longer toward the outer edge, for a lifted, elongated shape.

Works well with hybrid or mega volume

Squirrel

Longer toward the outer middle with a slightly wispy finish.

Works well with volume or hybrid

What to expect

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We look at your natural lashes, talk through the look you want and decide on length, curl and fullness.

  2. 02

    Prep

    Your lashes are cleansed, under-eye pads go on and your natural lashes are separated so each one can be treated alone.

  3. 03

    Application

    Lie back and close your eyes. Most people nap. Each extension is attached to a single natural lash.

  4. 04

    Aftercare

    Keep them dry for 24 hours, brush daily and book a fill every two to three weeks. We send you a one-page guide.
