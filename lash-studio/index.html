The Ritual

An hour (or three) entirely yours.

Unhurried, quiet and considered. From the moment you arrive, the details are ours to look after.

  1. i.

    Consultation

    We study your eye shape, lash health and lifestyle, then agree on the length, curl and density that suit you.

  2. ii.

    Mapping

    Your lash line is mapped by hand so every extension sits exactly where it should: no guesswork, no excess weight.

  3. iii.

    Application

    Rest back, close your eyes. Each extension is isolated, placed and sealed individually with medical-grade, low-fume adhesive.

  4. iv.

    Aftercare

    You leave with a simple routine and a fill schedule, and our door stays open for questions at any time.
