General maintenance

Keep your car on its factory maintenance schedule and catch small problems before they turn into big ones. We follow the maintenance intervals for your make and model.

Book general maintenance

What's included

  • Factory-scheduled service (30k / 60k / 90k miles)
  • Tune-ups, spark plugs and ignition
  • Air, cabin and fuel filters
  • Belts, hoses and coolant
  • Multi-point inspection with every visit

Signs you need it

  • You're due or past due on scheduled service
  • Rough idle or lower gas mileage
  • Check engine light
  • Buying a used car and want it inspected

Oil changes

The right oil for your engine and a new filter, plus a quick look at fluids, tires and anything that needs attention.

Book oil changes

What's included

  • Conventional, high-mileage and full synthetic
  • New oil filter
  • Fluid top-off
  • Tire pressure check
  • Reset of the oil-life monitor

Signs you need it

  • Oil change light or sticker reminder
  • Dark or low oil on the dipstick
  • Engine sounds louder than usual
  • It's been more than 5,000 miles

Brake jobs

Brakes are the one system you can't put off. We inspect the whole system and tell you what actually needs replacing.

Book brake jobs

What's included

  • Brake pads and shoes
  • Rotors and drums
  • Calipers and brake hoses
  • Brake fluid flush
  • ABS warning light diagnosis

Signs you need it

  • Squealing or grinding when you stop
  • Car pulls to one side under braking
  • Soft or spongy pedal
  • Steering wheel shakes when braking

Suspension work

Worn suspension makes a car ride rough, wear out tires early and handle poorly. We replace the worn parts and get it riding right.

Book suspension work

What's included

  • Shocks and struts
  • Control arms and ball joints
  • Tie rods and steering components
  • Bushings and sway bar links
  • Wheel bearings

Signs you need it

  • Clunking over bumps
  • Bouncy or floaty ride
  • Uneven tire wear
  • Car wanders or pulls on the freeway

A/C repair

In Las Vegas, a broken A/C isn't optional. We find the leak or the failed part instead of just recharging it and sending you off.

Book A/C repair

What's included

  • A/C performance check and leak test
  • Refrigerant evacuate and recharge
  • Compressors and clutches
  • Condensers, evaporators and hoses
  • Blend door and blower motor repair

Signs you need it

  • A/C blows warm or only cool at highway speed
  • Weak airflow from the vents
  • Strange smell from the vents
  • Clicking or squealing when the A/C kicks on

Electrical issues

Electrical problems take patience and the right tools. We trace the fault to its source instead of throwing parts at it.

Book electrical issues

What's included

  • Check engine light diagnosis
  • Batteries, starters and alternators
  • Parasitic battery drain testing
  • Wiring repair and shorts
  • Sensors, modules and lighting

Signs you need it

  • Car won't start or cranks slowly
  • Battery keeps dying
  • Warning lights on the dash
  • Lights, windows or gauges acting up
