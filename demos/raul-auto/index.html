Services

Full-service repair

Daily drivers, work trucks and family cars. Bring it in for routine service or for the problem nobody else could find.

General maintenance

Factory-scheduled service, tune-ups, filters, belts, hoses, fluids and multi-point inspections.

Oil changes

Conventional, high-mileage and full synthetic oil, with a new filter and a quick check of fluids and tires.

Brake jobs

Pads, rotors, calipers, lines and brake fluid flushes. If it squeals, grinds or pulls, we'll find out why.

Suspension work

Shocks, struts, control arms, ball joints, tie rods and bushings. Your car should ride smooth and track straight.

A/C repair

Leak checks, recharges, compressors, condensers and blend doors. Built for Las Vegas summers.

Electrical issues

No-starts, parasitic drains, wiring faults, check engine lights, sensors and modules, traced to the source.

We don't do body work

We're a mechanical and electrical shop. For dents, paint or collision repair, you'll need a body shop.

Questions

Common questions

Do you do body work?

No. We're a full mechanical and electrical shop, but we don't do body work, paint or collision repair.

Do you work with insurance and extended warranty companies?

Yes. We work directly with insurance companies and extended warranty companies on covered claims and repairs.

Can I finance my repair?

Yes. We offer financing through Affirm, Sunbit and Klarna, subject to approval.

My car won't start. Can you tow it in?

Yes. We partner with Los Crazies Towing to bring your vehicle to the shop.

Do you do engine swaps and fuel injection conversions?

Yes. Engine swaps, transmission swaps, carburetor-to-EFI conversions, lift kits and lowering kits are our specialty work.

What areas do you serve?

We're in Las Vegas and see customers from across the valley, including North Las Vegas, Henderson, Paradise and Spring Valley.
