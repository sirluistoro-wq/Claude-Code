Insurance · Warranty · Financing

Covered repairs.
Flexible payments.

We work directly with insurance and extended warranty companies, and offer financing through Affirm, Sunbit and Klarna.

Extended warranty

Warranty repairs

Have an extended warranty or vehicle service contract? Bring us your contract info. We diagnose the problem, contact the warranty company, and handle the paperwork for covered repairs.

  • We call the warranty company for you
  • Diagnosis and documentation for the claim
  • You pay only your deductible and anything not covered
Insurance

Insurance claims

For mechanical repairs covered by your insurance, we work with your adjuster directly, provide the estimate and photos they need, and keep you updated.

  • Estimates and photos for adjusters
  • Direct communication with your insurer
  • Mechanical and electrical repairs (no body work)
Financing

Fix it now, pay over time

A transmission or engine job shouldn't have to wait for payday. Apply with one of our financing partners and split the repair into payments.

Affirm

Pay over time with fixed monthly payments. You see the total cost up front.

Sunbit

Built for auto repair. Apply at the counter and get a decision in seconds.

Klarna

Split the cost into smaller payments over time.

Financing is subject to approval. Rates and terms are set by each lender.

Questions

Claims and payment

Do you work with insurance and extended warranty companies?

Yes. We work directly with insurance companies and extended warranty companies on covered claims and repairs.

Can I finance my repair?

Yes. We offer financing through Affirm, Sunbit and Klarna, subject to approval.

Do I have to approve the price before you start?

Yes. We diagnose the vehicle first and give you an estimate. No work starts until you approve it.
