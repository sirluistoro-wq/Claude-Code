Aftercare & FAQ

Make your setlast.

Good aftercare is the difference between a set that lasts one week and one that lasts four. Here's everything you need.

On this page The first 24 hoursDaily careDo & don'tFillsFAQ

The first 24 hours

The adhesive finishes curing over the first day. Keep things dry and gentle while it does.

  • 0–24 HRKeep lashes dry. No steam, sauna, hot showers on the face or sweaty workouts.
  • 0–48 HRSkip lash tints, facials and anything that heats the eye area.
  • NIGHT 1Sleep on your back if you can, or use a silk pillowcase.

Daily care

  • AMBrush gently with a clean spoolie, from the middle of the lashes outward.
  • PMCleanse with an oil-free lash foam and a soft brush. Rinse and pat dry.
  • WEEKLYSwap your spoolie for a clean one. Old brushes carry oil back onto lashes.

Do & don't

Do

  • Cleanse every day, especially if you wear makeup
  • Use oil-free skincare around the eyes
  • Book your fill every 2–3 weeks
  • Message me if a lash feels uncomfortable

Don't

  • Pull, pick or rub your extensions
  • Use oil-based removers or cleansing balms
  • Wear mascara on extensions
  • Use a lash curler

When to book a fill

You naturally shed a few lashes a day. Book a fill every 2 to 3 weeks, while at least 40% of your set remains. Under 40%, a new full set is needed.

Frequently asked

Will extensions damage my natural lashes?

Not when they're applied correctly. I match the weight of each extension to the strength of your natural lash, and isolate every one so lashes shed on their normal cycle.

How long does a full set take?

Classic sets take about 1 hr 45 min. Hybrid and volume take 2 to 2.5 hours. Fills are 45 to 75 minutes.

How long do extensions last?

Each extension falls out with the natural lash it's attached to, usually over 3 to 4 weeks. Fills every 2 to 3 weeks keep your set full.

Can I wear contacts?

Yes. Wear glasses to your appointment if you can, and bring your contact case so you can pop them back in after.

I have sensitive eyes. Can I still get lashes?

Usually, yes. Book the free patch test first. I also keep a sensitive-formula adhesive for clients who react to standard glue.

Can I get extensions while pregnant?

Many clients do. Hormones can change how long your set lasts, so check with your doctor and expect shorter retention.

What's your cancellation policy?

Change or cancel for free up to 48 hours before. Inside 48 hours, your deposit covers the reserved time. More than 15 minutes late may mean a shortened service.

How do I pay?

A $25 deposit online holds your spot. The balance is paid at the studio by card, Apple Pay or cash.
