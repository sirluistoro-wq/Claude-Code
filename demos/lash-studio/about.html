The artist & the studio

Hi, I'mMara.

Eight years, 6,000+ sets and one rule: your lashes should look like yours, only better.

Mara

My story

I lash the way I'd want my own lashes done.

I started lashing in a shared salon chair, working weekends while I trained. The thing that bothered me most was how often clients left with the same set as the person before them, whatever their eye shape.

Velour is the studio I wanted to sit in: one client at a time, a real consultation, and a lash map drawn for your eyes before a single extension goes on.

When I'm not lashing, I'm teaching retention classes to new artists or testing adhesives so you don't have to.

What I care about

The studio standard

The small things that decide whether your set lasts three weeks or one.

Healthy lashes first

Extension weight is matched to every natural lash. If your lashes can't hold a look, I'll tell you and suggest one they can.

Clinical-level hygiene

Sterilised tweezers, single-use brushes and pads, and a fresh bed cover for every client.

Quiet, private, one-on-one

No double-booking and no shared room. Just you, a weighted blanket and two hours to yourself.

Your appointment

What happens in the chair

A full set takes about two hours. Most clients fall asleep somewhere around step three.

STEP 1 · 10 MIN

Consult

We talk about your routine, sensitivities and the look you're after.

STEP 2 · 10 MIN

Map

I sketch your lash map on the eye pad: curl, lengths and direction, zone by zone.

STEP 3 · 90 MIN

Build

Each extension is isolated and bonded to one natural lash. Eyes closed, no stinging.

STEP 4 · 10 MIN

Reveal

Mirror moment, aftercare walkthrough, and your fill booked before you leave.
